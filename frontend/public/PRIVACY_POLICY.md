# Privacy Policy - KaushalChakra

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

KaushalChakra respects your privacy and complies with Indias Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## What We Collect and Why

- Profile Information (name, email, department, password hash): To create and secure your account, and to display your identity to matched partners after confirmation.
- Skill Claims (offered/wanted skills and levels): To run the cyclic matching engine.
- Availability Slots: To prefer cycles where participants share overlapping free times (tie-breaker only).
- Certificate Verification Data (issuer, verification ID/link): To verify expertise; only a boolean verified flag is shown to others, never the raw ID.
- Chat Messages: Scoped to the cycle, visible only to participants.
- Ratings and Reviews: Only aggregated scores are public.
- Task Descriptions: To enable task-based credit exchanges.
- Consent Timestamp (consentGivenAt): To record when you consented.

## Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent by contacting the administrator. Withdrawing consent will deactivate your account.

## Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active and for a limited period after deactivation to resolve disputes.

## Contact

For privacy queries, contact the KaushalChakra administrator via the in-app support channel.
